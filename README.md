# My Favorite Anime

My three favorite anime are **Overlord**, **Sword Art Online**, and **Tensura**.

---

## 1. Overlord
*Overlord* is about Ainz Ooal Gown he is a powerful player trapped in a fantasy world basically isekai.

- **Genre:** Action, Fantasy, Adventure
- **Favorite character:** Albedo.

## 2. Sword Art Online
*Sword Art Online* is about players that got trapped inside a virtual reality game.

- **Genre:** Action, Romance, Fantasy
- **Favorite characters:** Kirito and Asuna.

## 3. Tensura
*That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime* is about Rimuru Tempest as the title state he is reincarnated as a slime.

- **Genre:** Fantasy, Adventure, Comedy
- **Favorite character:** Testarossa previously know as blanc the pride white primodial.

---

### My Opinion
I enjoy these anime because they have amazing characters, exciting battles, interesting stories, and also it is my escape in reality.

> These anime make watching fantasy and adventure stories enjoyable!
